# Fantastic diseases

Fantastic diseases use the ordinary strategic health simulation. Their
organisms and toxins are invented, but their transmission, time courses,
functional losses, phenotype variation, and treatment response are physical.
The Humour chart is a lossy historical projection, never a causal model.

## Authoritative mechanics

| Disease | Invented biology and route | Coordinated course | Grounded response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mahrdruck | A mite or moth symbiont in shared straw releases a sleep-linked neuro-respiratory agent. The route is vermin and infested bedding, not a supernatural visit. | Neurologic loss precedes a coupled oxygenation trough; paralysis, chest pressure, and air hunger cluster after sleep. | Replace or isolate bedding through ordinary exposure prevention; skilled Physiology can monitor oxygenation. Poppy may reduce neurologic loss but its generic oxygenation hazard makes it a dangerous trade. |
| Nachzehrer wasting | A cadaver fungus forms an environmental spore reservoir after burial and can produce a delayed second exposure wave. Patients do not infect one another. Exhumation may increase dose. | Nutrition loss precedes inflammation and tissue damage; wasting and dark oral filaments emerge in sequence. | Avoid or contain disturbed grave material. Existing generic anti-inflammatory and supportive preparations affect the same meters regardless of diagnosis. |
| Bilwisschuss | A rye-gall organism sheds toxin-bearing hooked awns. It is an environmental exposure, not a projectile or spell. | Abrupt, often one-sided neurologic loss is followed by inflammation, contracture, and eye irritation. | Leave contaminated grain and clothing, wash exposed material, and use generic neurologic support. Poppy preparations retain their ordinary respiratory and renal adverse effects. |
| Kobeldunst | A living ore biofilm produces an organometallic vapour in confined mine air. It is exposure-bound and non-contagious. | Oxygenation falls first, followed by neurologic and renal-clearance loss; eyes burn before tremor and confusion become prominent. | Fresh air and ventilation stop further exposure. Physiology monitors the patient; generic hydration or neurologic preparations act only through their normal profiles. |

None has a disease-keyed cure, supernatural meter, or class gate. Herbalism
crafts preparations; Physiology recognizes patterns, administers them, and
monitors response. Environmental control uses the same route-prevention
affordances as ordinary disease.

## Diagnostic contract

The four courses deliberately coordinate existing private meters more strongly
than ordinary diseases. A high-Physiology observer who has authorized bedside
history can compare the *visible sequence* of quantized regional Humour
readings against public definitions. The scorer receives only that sequence,
including the actual timestamps and spacing of authorized observations. It
never receives the patient's infection identity, raw meters, or a reconstructed
sample for an unobserved time. A gap resets the sequence, and a self chart has
only its current snapshot. Before the authored minimum observation window, the
ordinary single-reading differential remains ambiguous. Incidental symptoms
and phenotype variation still create overlap.

Future outbreak, corpse, threat, and environment generators may use the open,
weighted physical evidence IDs `straw_mite_husks`, `black_grave_mould`,
`barbed_rye_galls`, and `iridescent_ore_biofilm`. These are evidence, not
hidden diagnosis lookups, and site constraints must remain explicit.

## Historical belief and adaptation notes

These notes describe inspiration, not authoritative biology.

### Mahrdruck / Alpdrücken

- **Attested term:** the *Frühneuhochdeutsches Wörterbuch* entry for
  [Mar](https://www.fwb-online.de/go/mar.s.0m_1709314623) records the nightmare
  being/name and cites Luther material from 1527–1530.
- **Place limitation:** the cited witnesses belong to Luther material and its
  Wittenberg milieu, but the dictionary entry does not establish an exact place
  for every witness. Later northern records from Oldenburg, Saterland, East
  Friesland, and Baltrum are collection evidence from later centuries, not
  proof of a local 1544 Mahr narrative.
- **Adaptation classification:** canonizing the name for the northern-Germany
  setting is a deliberate regional displacement of a period German term from
  an incompletely located source tradition, not a claim of local attestation.
- **Later variants:** pressure, paralysis, and northern European nightmare
  narratives appear in later collections, including the texts gathered in
  [The Mare in Scandinavian Belief](https://sites.pitt.edu/~dash/nightmare.html#mart).
  Those details are inspiration, not evidence that every motif was current in
  northern Germany in 1544.
- **Deliberate invention:** the bedding arthropod, symbiont, agent, and ordered
  neuro-respiratory course.

### Nachzehrer

- **Attested context:** Thomas Schürmann, “Der Nachzehrerglauben in
  Mitteleuropa,” *Ethnographisch-Archäologische Zeitschrift* 50 (2009),
  [article PDF](https://www.eaz-journal.org/index.php/eaz/article/download/851/907/2938),
  discusses corpse-chewing/epidemic reports around Silesia and Breslau in
  1516, Luther correspondence around 1540, and Freiberg around 1550.
- **Regional displacement:** this central/eastern German material reaches the
  northern-Germany game setting through travel; it is not presented as a
  universal local belief.
- **Deliberate invention:** the cadaver fungus, spores, delayed reservoir, oral
  filaments, and the possibility that opening a grave worsens exposure.
  Beheading is an in-world theory, not a causal mechanic.

### Bilwisschuss / Belewittenschnitt

- **Attested term:** the *Frühneuhochdeutsches Wörterbuch*
  [Bilwis entry](https://fwb-online.de/lemma/bilwis.s.1f) includes witnesses
  dated 1465, 1484, and before 1486.
- **Place limitation:** those witness dates alone do not establish a northern
  German place of attestation. Middle Low German and Lower Saxon forms such as
  *Belewitte* are later naming evidence; they do not make the staged disease a
  documented northern belief in 1544.
- **Adaptation classification:** the canonical northern use is a deliberate
  regional displacement from incompletely located medieval German witnesses;
  its local crop context is invented rather than presented as attested fact.
- **Inference:** combining those terms into one crop-demon explanation is
  cross-period interpretation, not one uniform 1544 doctrine.
- **Deliberate invention:** rye galls, toxin-bearing awns, and their staged
  focal neurologic course.
- **Rejected candidate:** the concrete Roggenmuhme material considered during
  research is too late for this use: a Saalfeld story is dated 1662 and a Mark
  Brandenburg publication 1816. It is not a canonical disease here.

### Kobeldunst / Bergmännlein

- **Attested context:** Georgius Agricola describes dangerous mine vapours and
  miners' accounts of mine spirits in *De re metallica* (1556), especially
  [Book VI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/38015/38015-h/38015-h).
  His *De animantibus subterraneis* (1549) is catalogued by the
  [Folger Shakespeare Library](https://catalog.folger.edu/record/367251).
- **Regional caution:** Saxon/Bohemian mining evidence is near-period context.
  Applying it to a Harz mine is plausible displacement; later developed Harz
  legend is not back-projected as 1544 testimony.
- **Deliberate invention:** the ore biofilm, organometallic vapour, and ordered
  oxygenation-neurologic-renal course.
